ENGLISH HISTORY • 1483

The Princes in the Tower

Two princes. One mystery.

Who Were They?

Edward V and his younger brother Richard were two English princes who disappeared in 1483.

Edward V

Edward became king at the age of 12 after his father died. He was never crowned.

Richard of York

Richard was Edward's younger brother. He joined him in the Tower of London.

What Happened?

The two boys were last seen in the Tower of London. Their fate remains unknown.

Their uncle, Richard III, is often suspected, but there is no definitive proof of who was responsible for their disappearance.

Timeline

April 1483: Edward IV dies.

May 1483: Edward V enters the Tower.

June 1483: Richard joins his brother.

July 1483: Richard III becomes king.
